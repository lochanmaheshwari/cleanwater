rules

  • Rank is the bid — nothing else. No ads, no algorithm, no votes.
  • New listings are whole US dollars. $5 minimum, $999,999 maximum, $1 increments.
  • Taking #1 costs at least $5 more than the current top bid.
  • Paying less than the #1 price still puts you on the board at whatever rank that bid can take.
  • Bids never expire, decay, or get refunded.
  • Equal bids stay in the order placed — the older bid keeps the higher rank.
  • Re-bidding the same URL or @handle adds to the existing total; pay only the difference. Never create a duplicate.
  • Anyone can fund a boost for any listing, not just its submitter.
  • Today's board ranks only what was spent in the last 24 hours.
  • Accepted: a product website or an X @handle.
  • Rejected: chat/invite links, link shorteners, adult content.
  • Strip all tracking query parameters. Normalise so site.com, www.site.com and site.com/?utm=x are one listing.
  • Outbound links carry rel="sponsored".
  • 75% of every bid is donated by you, directly to the LifeStraw Safe Water Fund. It never enters our account, and it is not refundable.
