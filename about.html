AI drinks. We pay it back.

Every pay-to-rank board keeps the money. This one keeps a quarter — and never even holds the rest.

Data centres run on water. Cooling the servers behind AI models evaporates tens of millions of gallons of clean freshwater every day.

When you claim a spot on savewater.tech, 75% of your money goes directly to Water.org via Every.org (501(c)(3) nonprofit). It goes straight from you to them. We never touch or hold it. The remaining 25% pays for the servers and platform development.

Through innovative micro-financing (WaterCredit), Water.org has reached over 70 million people with clean, safe household water and sanitation. Every dollar makes a multiplying, long-term impact.
