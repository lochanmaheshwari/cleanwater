AI drinks. We pay it back.

Every pay-to-rank board keeps the money. This one keeps a quarter — and never even holds the rest.

Data centres run on water. Cooling the infrastructure behind every model consumes it, and the industry is growing faster than anyone planned for.

When you claim a spot here, you donate 75% directly to Water.org via Every.org. It goes from you to them. We never see it. The other 25% pays for the servers and keeps things like this getting built.

One in four people on earth — 2.1 billion — still lack safely managed drinking water. This is a small way for an industry that runs on water to send some back.
