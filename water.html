What is Water.org?

Global Clean Water Nonprofit founded by Gary White & Matt Damon

Water.org is a global nonprofit organization dedicated to bringing safe water and sanitation to families living in poverty. Co-founded by social entrepreneur Gary White and actor/advocate Matt Damon, Water.org has transformed the international water sector by creating market-driven financial solutions that empower people with access to safe water for generations.

Instead of just digging isolated wells that often break down, Water.org breaks the systemic cycle of water poverty by working directly with local financial institutions, governments, and utilities to make household taps, rainwater harvesting, and safe toilets affordable for everyone.

Breaking the Cycle of Poverty

Every $5 donated provides reliable, safe clean water access that lasts for years. When clean water flows directly at home, water-borne illnesses drop by over 80% and childhood mortality plummets.

How Does Water.org Work? (WaterCredit)

Smart, sustainable, and multiplying impact

Millions of families in poverty already pay high costs in time and money to purchase water from vendor trucks or walk miles every day to collect dirty water. Water.org pioneered WaterCredit:

1.
Small, Affordable Micro-loans: Families take small loans ($50 - $250) to connect directly to the municipal water line, install household piping, or build safe toilets.
2.
99% Repayment Rate: Borrowers repay their loans with a 99% track record. Over 90% of borrowers are women, who gain hours of time back to work, start businesses, and send children to school.
3.
Evergreen Recycling of Capital: As loans are repaid, the capital is re-loaned to another family in need, multiplying the impact of every single dollar donated.
